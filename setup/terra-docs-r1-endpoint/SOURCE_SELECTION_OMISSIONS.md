## Publication decision

Public official URLs and this source/version/omission mapping may be proposed for publication after a coordinator checks attribution and privacy. Technical files, FTS archives, raw upstream source and full logs are **withheld** until another authorized model cross-reviews content/omissions and a rights/attribution review determines what may actually be redistributed. This matrix is not such approval.
